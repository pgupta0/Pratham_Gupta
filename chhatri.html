How it works

Bare tin cooks the room below

The roof is the hottest surface in the house. It soaks up sun all day and radiates it straight down onto the people inside.

Layer 1 · Reflective coat

Bounce the sun back to the sky

A white reflective coat sends up to 85–90% of sunlight back up before it turns into heat. Roof surfaces run up to 20°C cooler.

Layer 2 · Foil and air gap

Block what gets through

On thin tin, paint alone is not enough. A foil sheet with a still-air gap stops the hot metal from radiating into the room.

Layer 3 · Wind-driven vent

Pull the heat out after sunset

A turbine vent uses the wind, not electricity, to purge trapped hot air so nights cool down.

Layer 4 · Proof

Measured, not claimed

Every colony is logged against untreated homes. Each family gets its own temperature by text. Target: 2 to 5°C cooler, with zero electricity, and at least 2°C guaranteed.

Is this feasible in India today?

Yes. Every part already exists. We put them together.

Nothing here needs a new invention, an import or a subsidy that doesn't exist yet. It needs a model where someone pays, someone installs and someone proves the result.

The physics works

Indian field trials show cool roofs cut indoor temperatures by 2–5°C and roof surfaces by up to 20°C.

Materials are off the shelf

Reflective coatings cost ₹9–16 per sq ft, bubble-foil insulation ₹8–10 per sq ft, and turbine vents start around ₹1,100. All are made in India.

It needs little capital

The sponsor pays ₹7,000 when the roof is fitted, against a blended cost of about ₹7,300. The family's monthly fee becomes the margin.

Families can afford it

₹200 a month is about a basic mobile recharge. It is collected by UPI autopay or through the self-help group.

The workforce is there

Urban women's self-help groups already run savings, loans and door-to-door work in these colonies. Coating and fitting are learned on the job.

Payers are already looking

Companies must spend 2% of profits on CSR, and environment is an approved area. Telangana targets 300 sq km of cool roofs by 2028, and cities run heat action plans.

How we sell

  1. Sponsor first. No colony starts without a signed payer: a CSR fund, a city body or an employer. They buy verified degrees, not paint.
  2. Women's groups enrol. Self-help groups sign families up door to door. At 30% of a colony, the crew is dispatched.
  3. Sheds pay early bills. From month one, we sell cool roofs and vents to small factories and warehouses. They pay cash and see savings on their own meters.
  4. Proof sells the next colony. Families get their temperature by SMS. Sponsors get a monthly dashboard against untreated homes. Both become referrals.
  5. Get written into policy. By year 3, we aim to be a listed delivery partner in city heat action plans and state cool-roof budgets.

What gets harder to copy as we grow

Temperature data

Readings from every colony and its untreated neighbours let us price the guarantee better than anyone entering later.

Trust on the ground

Women's groups and crews who live in these colonies are the reason families sign up. They take years to build.

Sponsor contracts

Multi-year deals with cities, CSR funds and employers, renewed on verified results.

Falling cost

Bulk buying and faster crews cut the cost per roof every year, from about ₹7,800 to ₹6,800 in our plan.

What could go wrong

Six risks, each with a plan.

Because the sponsor pays for the roof when it is fitted, no single risk below can sink the model.

Families stop paying

Fees are collected by UPI autopay or through the self-help group. The plan already assumes only 85% is collected.

Sponsors pay late

We ask for 50% upfront when a colony is signed, and keep a working-capital credit line for the rest.

The monsoon stops work

Fitting runs October to May. The monsoon months go to enrolment, training and signing sponsors.

The coating wears off

Dust and weather lower reflectance, so a re-coat is built into year 4 and covered by the care plan.

Unsafe or asbestos roofs

Every roof is surveyed first. Crews never cut or drill asbestos, coat it only with safety kit, and skip roofs that are unsafe.

The 2°C guarantee is missed

Each colony is compared with matched untreated homes. If a home misses, we re-treat it at our cost.
