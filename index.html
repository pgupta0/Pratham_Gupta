02 BigBasketStrategic Initiative Intern · Apr to Jun 2026

Then I took that curiosity into a live inventory problem.

The question

Write-offs were visible; the reasons behind them were scattered. How could the team spot loss early enough to act before it repeated?

What I built

I learned the floor process, researched the inventory system and brought SKU-level root causes into daily monitoring so operations and buying teams could work from the same view.

What proved it ~₹20L

saved, with write-offs moving from 1.21% to 0.88% of sales. It strengthened my belief that better data and automation make action easier.

03 Himanshu Travel LineOperations, growth & key accounts · 2023 to 2025

Here I learned how to move a price-sensitive service towards quality.

The question

A bus operator serving schools, colleges, hospitals and B2C customers. The market was price-sensitive, but reliability mattered just as much.

What I built

I handled operations, growth and key accounts, adding GPS, cameras and a clearer service proposition alongside corporate pricing, routes, dispatch and process improvements.

What proved it ₹2.5Cr

in B2B revenue from two new contracts and five expanded fleets. Vehicle availability rose 26% as the operating system became more dependable.
